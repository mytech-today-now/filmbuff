Failure mode

The handlers move a clip file and then append the state transition to an append-only JSONL log. Their catch blocks roll the move back if appendRecords() returns an error, but process termination between the successful rename and the append bypasses that rollback.

Evidence or rationale

Testable assumption: a process stop between the awaited filesystem rename and the awaited append leaves the previous status as complete or approved while the file is at its _attemptN path. Evidence: cli/src/commands/video/reject.ts:74-93 and cli/src/commands/video/reopen.ts:84-104 order renameClipForRetry() before appendRecords(); rollback exists only in the catch path. cli/src/lib/status-file-manager.ts:274-298 appends to the JSONL file under a lock. openspec/changes/filmb-ai-p/specs/video-status-file/spec.md defines the status log as append-only and the clip naming convention. Confirm with fault injection after rename and before append, then inspect both the latest status record and clip paths.

Why this matters

After an abrupt process stop, the append-only status record and the clip location can describe different lifecycle states. Operators may retry, approve, reject, or generate against a path that no longer contains the current clip.

Recommended mitigation

Close the crash window with the smallest recoverable protocol that respects the append-only status log and preserves attempt clips. Inspect the shot state machine and current status contract before choosing an approach. Make each intermediate step detectable and idempotently reconcilable after restart; do not claim filesystem and JSONL writes are atomic together.

Failure mode

Title cards are enabled by default. When an individual title-card ffmpeg command fails, compile.ts catches every error, emits no warning, advances lastScene, and continues. The combined video can therefore be reported as complete without a requested scene title card.

Evidence or rationale

Testable assumption: a drawtext, codec, font, or title-card rendering failure reaches the empty catch and yields a successful combined output missing that card. Evidence: cli/src/commands/video/compile.ts:143 enables title cards unless explicitly disabled; lines 193-224 catch render errors without reporting them and then set lastScene; lines 240-263 proceed to assembly and viewer output. Confirm with a controlled ffmpeg stub that fails only the title-card invocation and succeeds for concat.

Why this matters

A successful compile message can conceal missing scene separators. Reviewers may receive an output that differs from the requested compile options without knowing why.

Recommended mitigation

Keep the existing no-title-cards option and avoid turning an expected optional failure into a silent success. On a failed requested card, either stop the compile with an actionable error or preserve the skip fallback while emitting a warning that names the scene and confirms the card was omitted. Make agent-mode output equally explicit.
