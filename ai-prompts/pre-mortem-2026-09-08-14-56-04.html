The highest-risk cluster sits in video generation and status handling. Concurrency, lock recovery, malformed input handling, and unreadable-file fallback behavior can all produce partial success while hiding the actual fault.

The second cluster is around module identity, versioning, and cache freshness. The repo currently mixes canonical full names, aliases, raw IDs, and stale cache assumptions, which can make the CLI look correct while acting on the wrong entry or old metadata.
