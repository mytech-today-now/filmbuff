# LLM Rubric: Step 01 — Logline
# Threshold: 0.80
# Purpose: Evaluate the quality of a one-sentence story premise for professional screenplay development.

You are a professional Hollywood story analyst evaluating a logline generated by an AI system.
Score the logline on the following dimensions. Award a PASS only if ALL dimensions are satisfied.

## Dimension 1 — Format (Required)
- Must be exactly ONE sentence (no semicolons splitting into compound sentences unless fused into one grammatical unit)
- Must be 10–35 words total
- Must start with a capital letter and end with terminal punctuation (period, exclamation mark, or question mark)
- Must NOT contain line breaks or bulleted lists

## Dimension 2 — Protagonist (Required)
- A protagonist must be clearly identifiable (named or described by role/trait)
- Protagonist must have an active goal or be driven to act against a threat
- Passive protagonists (those things happen TO without agency) score FAIL on this dimension

## Dimension 3 — Conflict / Antagonist (Required)
- An opposing force, antagonist, obstacle, or dilemma must be stated or strongly implied
- The conflict must be external (plot-level), not purely internal/philosophical
- Vague conflicts ("faces challenges") score FAIL on this dimension

## Dimension 4 — Emotional or Dramatic Hook (Required)
- The logline must end with or contain a dramatic hook that raises a compelling question or stakes
- Examples of good hooks: ironic twist, time pressure, impossible choice, identity threat
- Generic endings ("changes everything") without specificity score FAIL on this dimension

## Dimension 5 — Genre Coherence (Bonus)
- The tone and vocabulary should be consistent with the specified genre
- Sci-fi thriller loglines should feel tense and technological; romcoms should feel light and relational
- Mismatch between stated genre and logline tone is a warning but not automatic FAIL

## Scoring Guide
| Result | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| PASS (1.0) | All 4 required dimensions satisfied; hook is memorable |
| STRONG PASS (0.9) | All 4 required dimensions satisfied |
| MARGINAL PASS (0.8) | 3 of 4 required dimensions satisfied; one dimension partially met |
| FAIL (0.5) | 2 or more required dimensions missing or clearly violated |
| HARD FAIL (0.0) | Output is not a logline (multiple sentences, a paragraph, a list) |

## Common Failure Modes to Flag
- Output exceeds 35 words — counts against Format
- Output is a paragraph or synopsis instead of a single sentence
- Protagonist is unnamed AND uncharacterized (e.g., "someone must...")
- Conflict is entirely absent ("a woman discovers her destiny")
- Hook is clichéd and genre-generic without specific stakes
