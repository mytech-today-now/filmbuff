# SPACE COMMIES

## LOGLINE
On a forgettable Starfleet vessel, a Captain decides to space some communist prisoners. Her First Officer objects on procedural grounds, but the Captain's logic is so sound that he eventually agrees spacing commies is just good policy. Dark absurdist parody of Star Trek's moral certainty meets Cold War patriotism.

**Runtime:** 3:30 | **Tone:** Deadpan patriotic absurdism | **Hook:** "I'm not going to follow [X]"

---

## THE WORLD

Generic Starfleet bridge. NOT the Enterprise. NOT any named ship. This is "USS [REDACTED]" or "Starfleet Vessel #4,782"—one of those forgettable ships where terrible things happen off-screen while Kirk is making out with aliens.

**Set:** Captain's chair center, tactical station, helm, viewscreen. Visible airlock door stage left.

---

## CHARACTERS

**THE CAPTAIN** (Host or strong featured player)
Unhinged authoritarian with perfect deadpan delivery. Never raises her voice. Completely unreasonable with total confidence. Generic "tough captain" archetype—could be anyone.

**THE FIRST OFFICER** (Strong straight man)
Voice of reason who slowly breaks. Arc: Reasonable → Desperate → Defeated → Converted. Generic "concerned XO" type.

**THE ENGINEER** (High energy)
Way too excited about murder. Enthusiastic enabler. "I'll get the airlock ready!" Generic "hot-headed engineer."

**THE ENSIGN** (Young cast member)
Cheerful complicity in war crimes. Baby-faced yes-man. Generic "eager operations officer."

## THE SKETCH

### COLD OPEN (0:00-0:30)
Bridge. Red alert lights. Captain in chair, crew at stations.

**FIRST OFFICER:** Captain, we've apprehended the Space Communists who were redistributing replicator rations.

**CAPTAIN:** Excellent. Prepare to jettison them out the airlock.

**FIRST OFFICER:** Captain, Starfleet regulations require we beam them to a rehabilitation colony.

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow Starfleet regulations.

**FIRST OFFICER:** That's... that's murder.

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow morality.

**ENGINEER:** *(enthusiastic)* I'll get the airlock ready!

**ENSIGN:** *(cheerful)* Depressurization sequence standing by, Captain!

### THE ESCALATION (0:30-2:00)

**FIRST OFFICER:** Captain, this violates the Prime Directive!

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow the Prime Directive.

**FIRST OFFICER:** The Geneva Convention!

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow the Geneva Convention.

**FIRST OFFICER:** The Khitomer Accords! The Treaty of Algeron!

**CAPTAIN:** Nope. Not following those either.

**FIRST OFFICER:** *(desperate)* Captain, you can't just ignore the laws of physics!

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow the laws of physics.

**ENGINEER:** Actually, Captain, the laws of physics will help us space them faster.

**CAPTAIN:** See? Physics is on my side.

**FIRST OFFICER:** This goes against the laws of God!

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow the laws of God.

**FIRST OFFICER:** The laws of man! The laws of logic!

**CAPTAIN:** Especially not logic.

**ENSIGN:** Captain, the communists are asking if they can at least have a trial.

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow due process.

### THE TWISTED LOGIC (2:00-2:30)

**FIRST OFFICER:** Captain, WHY are you doing this?

**CAPTAIN:** *(calm, measured)* Commander, if we beam them to a planet, they'll simply take over that planet. Then they'll use it as a base to spread their ideology. Before you know it, they've conquered the entire galaxy. But if we jettison them into space... the problem is solved. Permanently.

**FIRST OFFICER:** That's... that's...

**ENGINEER:** Brilliant!

**ENSIGN:** So efficient!

**FIRST OFFICER:** *(slowly)* Captain... I hate to say this, but... I see your point.

**CAPTAIN:** Thank you, Commander.

**FIRST OFFICER:** If we show mercy, we're just enabling future conquest.

**CAPTAIN:** Exactly. The needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few.

### THE BUTTON (2:30-3:30)

**ENSIGN:** Captain, incoming transmission from Starfleet Command.

**CAPTAIN:** On screen.

*[Viewscreen shows Admiral]*

**ADMIRAL:** Captain of the [REDACTED], we've received reports you're about to space some prisoners.

**CAPTAIN:** That's correct, Admiral.

**ADMIRAL:** That's a clear violation of—

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow Starfleet Command.

**ADMIRAL:** Captain, I'm ordering you to—

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow orders.

*[Captain cuts transmission]*

**ENGINEER:** Airlock ready, Captain!

**CAPTAIN:** Proceed.

*[Sound effect: Airlock opening, whoosh of decompression]*

**ENSIGN:** Captain, we're receiving a distress call from a nearby ship. They say they're being attacked by... Space Fascists.

**CAPTAIN:** Beam them aboard immediately. We'll give them sanctuary.

**FIRST OFFICER:** Captain, isn't that hypocritical?

**CAPTAIN:** I'm not going to follow consistency.

*[Blackout]*

## WHY IT WORKS

- **Catchphrase escalation:** "I'm not going to follow [X]" builds from reasonable to absurd
- **Crew enthusiasm:** Supporting characters are way too excited about atrocities
- **Conversion arc:** Voice of reason becomes complicit
- **Hypocritical twist:** Immediately contradicts herself
- **Meta-humor:** The "other ship" concept—this is happening where the show ISN'T
- **Timeless satire:** Authoritarian logic + Star Trek idealism
