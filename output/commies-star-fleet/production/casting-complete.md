## Casting Notes & Considerations

### Performance Direction Provided

**Tone Guidance:**
- This is deadpan absurdism, not broad comedy
- Play everything seriously within the absurd logic
- No winking at the audience
- Emotional authenticity is key

**Catchphrase Delivery:**
- "I'm not going to follow [X]" must be consistent
- Same calm, measured delivery each time
- No escalation in volume, only in absurdity of target

**Physical Comedy:**
- Engineer's enthusiasm is physical but not cartoonish
- First Officer's desperation builds through body language
- Ensign's cheerfulness is in posture and demeanor

### Accessibility & Inclusion

**Casting Flexibility:**
- All roles gender-flexible (as specified)
- Age ranges respected
- Diverse casting encouraged
- No specific physical requirements beyond performance capability
