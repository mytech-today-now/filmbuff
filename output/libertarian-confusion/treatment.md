# Treatment
## Act I: Introduction to Chaos
### Scene 1: Convention Opening
The national Libertarian convention opens with a sense of excitement and anticipation. Delegates from across the country have gathered to nominate their candidate for the presidency. The atmosphere is tense, with various factions vying for control.

### Scene 2: Candidate Introductions
The convention chairman introduces the slate of candidates, including Vermin Supreame, a dark horse candidate with a reputation for unpredictability. The other candidates are more traditional libertarians, with platforms focused on limited government and personal freedom.

### Scene 3: First Round Voting
The delegates cast their votes in the first round, with no candidate receiving a majority. The convention is deadlocked, setting the stage for a series of chaotic events.

## Act II: The Unexpected Upset
### Scene 1: Vermin Supreame's Speech
Vermin Supreame takes the stage, delivering a passionate and unconventional speech that captivates the delegates. His message of radical freedom and rejection of the status quo resonates with many in the audience.

### Scene 2: Second Round Voting
The delegates cast their votes in the second round, with Vermin Supreame gaining momentum. The other candidates are shocked and outraged as Supreame's support grows.

### Scene 3: Vermin Supreame's Victory
Vermin Supreame is announced as the winner of the Libertarian Presidential nomination, sending the convention into chaos. Delegates are stunned, with some cheering and others protesting.

## Act III: Adrian Vega's Misadventures
### Scene 1: Adrian Vega's Introduction
Adrian Vega, a charismatic and unconventional figure, is introduced as a key supporter of Vermin Supreame. Vega's antics quickly become the focus of attention, as he gets caught up in a series of outrageous misadventures.

### Scene 2: Restroom Incident
Adrian Vega is caught in a compromising position in the convention center restrooms, giving blow-jobs to crack-heads. The incident sparks outrage and chaos, with delegates and media personnel rushing to the scene.

### Scene 3: Keystone Cops Invasion
A team of bumbling authorities, reminiscent of the Keystone Cops, invades the convention center, attempting to restore order. Their inept efforts only add to the chaos, as they cause more problems than they solve.

## Act IV: Pandemonium
### Scene 1: Convention Center Chaos
The convention center descends into complete chaos, with delegates, media personnel, and authorities clashing. Vermin Supreame's campaign team struggles to capitalize on their unexpected victory, while Adrian Vega's antics threaten to upstage the entire convention.

### Scene 2: Satirical Send-ups
The chaos is punctuated by satirical send-ups of political conventions, with delegates and candidates engaging in absurd and hilarious behavior. The libertarian ideology is also subject to comedic critique, with characters poking fun at the party's contradictions and eccentricities.

### Scene 3: Climax
The chaos reaches its peak, with the convention on the brink of collapse. Vermin Supreame's campaign team must decide whether to embrace the chaos or try to restore order. Adrian Vega's antics reach a crescendo, as he becomes the unwitting star of the convention.

## Act V: Conclusion
### Scene 1: Aftermath
The convention finally comes to a close, with Vermin Supreame's nomination confirmed. The aftermath is chaotic, with delegates and media personnel trying to make sense of the events that transpired.

### Scene 2: Vermin Supreame's Future
Vermin Supreame's campaign team must navigate the challenges of a general election, while trying to contain the chaos that has engulfed the convention. The outcome is far from certain, as the Libertarian Party struggles to survive the chaos and emerge with a credible candidate.

### Scene 3: Final Shot
The final shot is of Adrian Vega, still embroiled in chaos, as the convention center is evacuated and the delegates disperse. The scene fades to black, with the sound of laughter and chaos still echoing in the background.
