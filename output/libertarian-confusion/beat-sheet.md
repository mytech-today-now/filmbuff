# Beat Sheet
## Opening Image
* Introduce the national Libertarian convention, showcasing the excitement and anticipation of the delegates

## Theme Stated
* Establish the theme of chaos and unpredictability in the pursuit of freedom and political power

## Set-Up
* Introduce the main characters, including Vermin Supreame and Adrian Vega
* Establish the setting of the convention center and the various factions vying for control

## Catalyst
* Vermin Supreame delivers a passionate and unconventional speech, captivating the delegates and setting the stage for chaos

## Debate
* Delegates are torn between supporting Vermin Supreame and the more traditional libertarian candidates

## Break into Two
* Vermin Supreame's speech sparks a chain reaction of events, leading to his unexpected victory and the chaos that ensues

## B Story
* Adrian Vega's misadventures begin, including the notorious incident in the convention center restrooms

## Fun and Games
* The convention center descends into chaos, with delegates, media personnel, and authorities clashing
* Satirical send-ups of political conventions and comedic critiques of libertarian ideology

## Midpoint
* The Keystone Cops-style invasion of the convention center, adding to the chaos and hilarity

## Bad Guys Close In
* The authorities and traditional libertarian factions attempt to restore order and undermine Vermin Supreame's campaign

## All is Lost
* The convention is on the brink of collapse, with Vermin Supreame's campaign team struggling to capitalize on their unexpected victory

## Dark Night of the Soul
* Adrian Vega's antics reach a crescendo, threatening to upstage the entire convention and undermine Vermin Supreame's chances

## Break into Three
* Vermin Supreame's campaign team must decide whether to embrace the chaos or try to restore order

## Finale
* The chaos reaches its peak, with the convention on the brink of collapse
* Vermin Supreame's nomination is confirmed, and the aftermath is chaotic

## Final Image
* The convention center is evacuated, and the delegates disperse, with Adrian Vega still embroiled in chaos as the scene fades to black
