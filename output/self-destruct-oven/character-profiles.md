# Character Profiles - Self-Destruct Oven

## Character 1: BRENDA HARTFORD
**Role**: Chief Marketing Officer (Executive)  
**Age**: 45-55  
**Accent/Speech Pattern**: Mid-Atlantic corporate, overly enthusiastic  
**Personality Archetype**: The Spin Doctor  

**Key Traits**:
- Sees every disaster as a "branding opportunity"
- Uses marketing jargon excessively ("synergy," "pivot," "disrupt")
- Maintains toxic positivity no matter how bad things get
- Physically animated - lots of hand gestures

**Motivation**: 
- Wants to rebrand the fires as a premium feature
- Believes any publicity is good publicity
- Desperate to avoid a product recall

**Memorable Quirk**: 
- Keeps referring to house fires as "thermal events" and "unplanned home renovations"
- Has a catchphrase: "That's not a bug, that's a FEATURE!"

**Sample Line**: 
"Folks, folks, folks - we're not selling ovens that burn down houses. We're selling ovens that create opportunities for home improvement!"

---

## Character 2: DEREK CHEN
**Role**: VP of Product Safety (Middle Management)  
**Age**: 35-40  
**Accent/Speech Pattern**: Nervous, fast-talking, slight stutter when stressed  
**Personality Archetype**: The Whistleblower  

**Key Traits**:
- Actually cares about safety
- Constantly interrupted or ignored
- Has all the data showing the problem
- Increasingly desperate as meeting progresses

**Motivation**: 
- Wants to recall the product and fix the issue
- Trying to save his job while doing the right thing
- Fears legal liability

**Memorable Quirk**: 
- Keeps pulling out charts and statistics that everyone ignores
- Stress-eats during the meeting
- Phone keeps buzzing with news alerts about fires

**Sample Line**: 
"I'm sorry, but when 40% of our ovens have caused structural fires, that's not a feature, that's a—" [gets cut off]

---

## Character 3: RONALD "RON" PEMBERTON III
**Role**: CEO  
**Age**: 60-70  
**Accent/Speech Pattern**: Old-money New England, slow and deliberate  
**Personality Archetype**: The Oblivious Executive  

**Key Traits**:
- Completely disconnected from reality
- Doesn't understand the product or the problem
- More concerned with golf schedule than fires
- Confuses basic facts constantly

**Motivation**: 
- Wants the meeting to end quickly
- Doesn't want to cancel his tee time
- Trusts his team to "handle it"

**Memorable Quirk**: 
- Keeps calling the oven a "microwave" or "toaster"
- Falls asleep briefly during the meeting
- Thinks they make refrigerators

**Sample Line**: 
"So what you're telling me is our microwaves are... too hot? Isn't that what microwaves do? Heat things?"

## Character 4: JESSICA RAMIREZ
**Role**: Junior Associate / Assistant  
**Age**: 25-30  
**Accent/Speech Pattern**: Valley Girl uptalk, overly cheerful  
**Personality Archetype**: The Yes-Man  

**Key Traits**:
- Agrees with whoever spoke last
- Takes notes on everything, even absurd statements
- Desperate to impress superiors
- No moral compass, just wants to climb ladder

**Motivation**: 
- Wants a promotion
- Will support any position that helps her career
- Terrified of being blamed

**Memorable Quirk**: 
- Literally writes down "fire = feature" in her notes
- Keeps offering to get people coffee
- Uses "literally" incorrectly constantly

**Sample Line**: 
"I literally think Brenda is literally right - this is literally a game-changer for the brand!"

## Character 5: MAURICE LEBLANC
**Role**: Head of Legal  
**Age**: 50-60  
**Accent/Speech Pattern**: French accent, speaks in legalese  
**Personality Archetype**: The Jargon Machine  

**Key Traits**:
- Speaks only in legal terminology
- Finds loopholes in everything
- Morally flexible
- Very calm, almost bored

**Motivation**: 
- Wants to avoid lawsuits
- Finds legal justification for calling it a feature
- Protects the company at all costs

**Memorable Quirk**: 
- Refers to everything as "alleged" even when it's obviously true
- Has a legal precedent for everything, no matter how absurd
- Pronounces "oven" with French accent ("oh-VAHN")

**Sample Line**: 
"Technically, ze oven did not start ze fire. Ze house was already... how you say... flammable. Zis is user error, non?"

## Character 6: TODD BRINKMAN
**Role**: VP of Engineering  
**Age**: 40-50  
**Accent/Speech Pattern**: Flat Midwestern, monotone, technical  
**Personality Archetype**: The Nervous Subordinate  

**Key Traits**:
- Knows exactly what's wrong but won't say it directly
- Hides behind technical jargon
- Sweats visibly
- Tries to stay neutral

**Motivation**: 
- Doesn't want to be blamed for the design flaw
- Wants to keep his job
- Hopes someone else will make the decision

**Memorable Quirk**: 
- Keeps saying "Well, technically..." before every statement
- Wipes forehead with handkerchief constantly
- Refers to fires as "exothermic reactions"

**Sample Line**: 
"Well, technically, the self-cleaning cycle does reach temperatures of 900 degrees Fahrenheit, which is, technically, hot enough to ignite most common household materials, so, technically..."

## Character Dynamics

### Power Hierarchy
1. Ron (CEO) - highest authority, least competent
2. Brenda (CMO) - most influential, drives the "feature" narrative
3. Maurice (Legal) - enabler, provides cover
4. Derek (Safety) - opposition, gets overruled
5. Todd (Engineering) - fence-sitter, won't commit
6. Jessica (Assistant) - lowest rank, most enthusiastic

### Key Conflicts
- **Derek vs. Brenda**: Safety vs. Marketing
- **Derek vs. Everyone**: Voice of reason vs. corporate groupthink
- **Ron vs. Reality**: Oblivious leader vs. actual crisis

### Comedy Pairings
- Brenda and Jessica feed off each other's enthusiasm
- Maurice provides legal cover for Brenda's absurd claims
- Todd and Derek share knowing glances but Todd won't back him up
- Ron's confusion creates chaos that Brenda exploits

## Casting Notes
- All characters should be distinct enough for audience to track
- Accents should be clear but not caricatures
- Physical comedy opportunities for each character
- Each character gets at least one big laugh moment
