# Delta: Self-Destruct Oven SNL Skit

## Changes from Original Prompt

### Additions

#### 1. Structural Formalization
- **Added**: Three-act structure with specific timing for each act
- **Rationale**: Ensures proper pacing and SNL format compliance
- **Impact**: Provides clear framework for writers

#### 2. Character Specifications
- **Added**: Minimum character count (4-6)
- **Added**: Required character attributes (name, role, personality, accent, motivation)
- **Rationale**: Original prompt mentioned "distinct personalities and accents" but didn't specify how many or what details
- **Impact**: Ensures character depth and variety

#### 3. Comedy Metrics
- **Added**: Minimum laugh opportunities (8-10)
- **Added**: Requirement for at least 2 "big" laughs
- **Rationale**: Quantifies "comedic" requirement from original prompt
- **Impact**: Sets measurable quality standards

#### 4. Production Constraints
- **Added**: Set, prop, costume, and special effects specifications
- **Rationale**: Makes the spec production-ready
- **Impact**: Ensures feasibility for SNL production

#### 5. Thematic Depth
- **Added**: Specific satire targets (corporate culture, modern technology, consumer culture)
- **Added**: Tone guidelines (absurdist, cynical, energetic, grounded)
- **Rationale**: Expands on "poke fun at corporate America" and "ridiculousness of modern technology"
- **Impact**: Provides clearer creative direction

#### 6. Acceptance Criteria
- **Added**: Checklist of deliverable requirements
- **Rationale**: Creates clear definition of "done"
- **Impact**: Enables objective quality assessment

### Clarifications

#### 1. Runtime Precision
- **Original**: "2.5 - 4 minute"
- **Clarified**: Approximately 2.5-4 pages of script, with act-by-act timing breakdowns
- **Rationale**: Script length correlates to performance time

#### 2. "Surprising and Thought-Provoking" Ending
- **Original**: General requirement
- **Clarified**: "Surprising twist or revelation" + "Thought-provoking conclusion" + "Final comedic button"
- **Rationale**: Breaks down vague requirement into actionable components

#### 3. SNL Style
- **Original**: "Written in the style of Saturday Night Live"
- **Clarified**: Specific format requirements, pacing notes, humor types, and production considerations
- **Rationale**: "SNL style" is subjective; specification provides concrete guidelines

#### 4. Corporate Satire
- **Original**: "Poke fun at corporate America"
- **Clarified**: Specific targets (spin over substance, profit over safety, groupthink, marketing euphemisms)
- **Rationale**: Focuses satirical intent on specific behaviors

## Risk Mitigations

### Original Prompt Risks
1. **Risk**: "Distinct personalities" could be superficial
   - **Mitigation**: Required motivation and memorable moment for each character

2. **Risk**: "Surprising ending" could feel forced
   - **Mitigation**: Required ending to "recontextualize the sketch"

3. **Risk**: Satire could be too broad or toothless
   - **Mitigation**: Specific satire targets and tone guidelines

4. **Risk**: Runtime could be off
   - **Mitigation**: Act-by-act timing breakdown and page count guidance
