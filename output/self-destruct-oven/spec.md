# Specification: Self-Destruct Oven SNL Skit

## Technical Requirements

### Format
- **Type**: Screenplay/Script
- **Style**: Saturday Night Live sketch format
- **Length**: 2.5 - 4 minutes (approximately 2.5-4 pages)
- **File Format**: Markdown or standard screenplay format

### Content Structure

#### 1. Header
- Title: "Self-Destruct Oven" or creative variant
- Setting description
- Character list with brief descriptions

#### 2. Characters (Minimum 4-6)
Each character must include:
- **Name**: Clear, memorable
- **Role**: Executive, middle management, engineer, etc.
- **Personality**: Distinct archetype (yes-man, whistleblower, spin doctor, etc.)
- **Accent/Speech Pattern**: Specified (Southern, New York, Valley Girl, corporate jargon, etc.)
- **Motivation**: What they want from the meeting

#### 3. Scene Structure

##### Act 1: Setup (30-60 seconds)
- Establish boardroom setting
- Introduce the problem: self-cleaning ovens catching fire
- Present the central conflict: "feature" vs "bug"

##### Act 2: Escalation (90-150 seconds)
- Debate intensifies with increasingly absurd arguments
- Characters reveal their distinct personalities
- Corporate doublespeak reaches peak absurdity
- Include at least 2-3 major comedic beats
- Reference real incidents/data (fictional but plausible)

##### Act 3: Resolution (30-60 seconds)
- Surprising twist or revelation
- Thought-provoking conclusion
- Final comedic button

#### 4. Dialogue Requirements
- **Pacing**: Rapid-fire exchanges typical of SNL
- **Humor Types**: 
  - Situational comedy
  - Character-based humor
  - Satirical commentary
  - Physical comedy opportunities (optional)
- **Catchphrases**: At least one memorable recurring line
- **Jargon**: Corporate buzzwords used satirically

#### 5. Stage Directions
- Clear blocking for actors
- Props needed (minimal: table, chairs, presentation materials)
- Key physical actions
- Timing notes for comedic pauses

### Thematic Requirements

#### Satire Targets
1. **Corporate Culture**
   - Spin over substance
   - Profit over safety
   - Groupthink and yes-men
   - Marketing euphemisms

2. **Modern Technology**
   - Over-engineering simple products
   - "Smart" features that create problems
   - Planned obsolescence

3. **Consumer Culture**
   - Blind acceptance of product flaws
   - Marketing manipulation

#### Tone Guidelines
- **Absurdist**: Situations escalate beyond realism
- **Cynical**: Expose corporate hypocrisy
- **Energetic**: Fast-paced, high energy
- **Grounded**: Relatable despite absurdity

### Quality Standards

#### Comedy Metrics
- Minimum 8-10 laugh opportunities
- At least 2 "big" laughs
- Consistent comedic escalation
- Payoff justifies setup

#### Character Depth
- Each character has at least one memorable moment
- Distinct voice for each character
- No character is purely exposition

#### Social Commentary
- Clear satirical message
- Relevant to contemporary issues
- Thought-provoking without being preachy

### Production Considerations
- **Set**: Single location (boardroom)
- **Props**: Minimal, easily sourced
- **Costumes**: Business attire
- **Special Effects**: None required (fire references are verbal)
- **Cast Size**: 4-6 actors

### Constraints
- Must be performable on SNL stage
- No offensive content beyond SNL standards
- Accessible humor (no niche technical knowledge required)
- Self-contained story (no prior knowledge needed)

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Runtime: 2.5-4 minutes when performed
- [ ] Character count: 4-6 distinct characters
- [ ] Each character has unique accent/personality
- [ ] Clear three-act structure
- [ ] Surprising ending that recontextualizes the sketch
- [ ] Satirizes corporate culture effectively
- [ ] Written in SNL house style
- [ ] Includes stage directions and timing notes
- [ ] Dialogue is performable and natural
- [ ] Ending is both funny and thought-provoking
