# Stage Directions and Blocking Notes
## Self-Destruct Oven - SNL Sketch

---

## SET DESIGN

### Main Elements
- **Conference table**: Long, seats 6 (3 per side + 1 at head)
- **Chairs**: 6 executive office chairs
- **Presentation screen**: Behind head of table (for slides)
- **Side table**: With coffee service (practical)
- **Door**: Stage right (for evacuation)

### Set Dressing
- Folders and papers on table
- Coffee cups (practical - for spilling)
- Water glasses
- Presentation clicker for Brenda
- Tablets/phones for characters

### Technical Requirements
- Fire alarm sound effect (loud, realistic)
- Red flashing lights (for alarm)
- Smoke effect (minimal, near door in Act 3)
- Presentation slides (can be projected or implied)

---

## CHARACTER BLOCKING

### Initial Positions (Act 1 Opening)

**RON** (CEO)
- Position: Head of table, center stage
- Posture: Leaning back, eyes closed initially
- Props: Coffee cup in hand

**BRENDA** (CMO)
- Position: Standing stage left by presentation screen
- Posture: Energetic, animated
- Props: Presentation clicker

**DEREK** (Safety VP)
- Position: Seated stage right, closest to Ron
- Posture: Hunched over papers, tense
- Props: Stack of folders, phone (buzzes frequently)

**MAURICE** (Legal)
- Position: Seated stage left, across from Derek
- Posture: Relaxed, leaning back
- Props: Briefcase, reading glasses

**TODD** (Engineering VP)
- Position: Seated stage right, next to Derek
- Posture: Nervous, fidgeting
- Props: Handkerchief (constantly wiping forehead), tablet

**JESSICA** (Junior Associate)
- Position: Seated stage left, furthest from Ron
- Posture: Perched on edge of seat, eager
- Props: Tablet, stylus, phone (for filming in Act 3)

## KEY BLOCKING MOMENTS

### ACT 1

**Beat 1: Ron Wakes Up**
- ACTION: Ron's head snaps up suddenly
- TIMING: On Brenda's "strategic alignment session"
- CAMERA: Close-up on Ron's confused face

**Beat 2: Derek's Presentation**
- ACTION: Derek stands, clicks remote
- BLOCKING: Moves to screen, points at graph
- REACTION SHOTS: Jessica drops stylus, Todd wipes forehead
- TIMING: Hold on graph for audience to read "40%"

**Beat 3: "That's Not a Bug, That's a FEATURE!"**
- ACTION: Brenda moves to center stage
- BLOCKING: Sweeping arm gesture on "FEATURE"
- REACTION SHOTS: All characters react differently
  - Jessica: Immediately starts typing
  - Derek: Slow head drop to hands
  - Todd: Wipes forehead
  - Maurice: Slight smile
  - Ron: Still confused

---

### ACT 2

**Beat 4: Brenda's Pacing**
- ACTION: Brenda paces behind her side of table
- BLOCKING: Gestures get bigger as she gets more excited
- TIMING: Constant movement creates energy

**Beat 5: Derek's Phone Buzzing**
- ACTION: Phone buzzes on table (vibrate sound)
- BLOCKING: Derek picks up, reads, face falls
- TIMING: Happens 3 times throughout Act 2
- CAMERA: Close-up on phone screen showing fire alerts

**Beat 6: Maurice's Legal Precedent**
- ACTION: Maurice stands calmly, gestures philosophically
- BLOCKING: Moves around table slowly while speaking
- TIMING: Deliberate, contrasts with Brenda's energy

**Beat 7: Todd's Technical Jargon**
- ACTION: Todd stands nervously, wipes forehead
- BLOCKING: Speaks faster and faster, wiping increases
- TIMING: Build to Derek's interruption
- PHYSICAL: Handkerchief gets more frantic

**Beat 8: Rebranding Session**
- ACTION: Everyone except Derek leans in, engaged
- BLOCKING: Rapid-fire suggestions, overlapping slightly
- TIMING: Fast-paced, building energy
- CAMERA: Quick cuts between speakers

**Beat 9: Derek Slams Folder**
- ACTION: Derek stands, slams folder on table (loud)
- BLOCKING: Leans forward on table, impassioned
- TIMING: Silence after slam for impact
- REACTION SHOTS: Everyone freezes, stares
- PHYSICAL: Coffee cup jumps from impact

**Beat 10: "Are We Still Talking About the Toaster?"**
- ACTION: Ron raises hand like student
- BLOCKING: Everyone's heads turn to him in unison
- TIMING: Beat of silence before Derek sits
- PHYSICAL: Derek's slow, defeated sit

---

### ACT 3

**Beat 11: Fire Alarm**
- ACTION: Alarm blares, red lights flash
- BLOCKING: Everyone looks up simultaneously
- TIMING: Sudden, jarring
- SOUND: Loud enough to be startling

**Beat 12: Evacuation Chaos**
- ACTION: Everyone grabs belongings, stands
- BLOCKING: Controlled chaos - papers flying, coffee spilling
- TIMING: Frantic but choreographed
- PHYSICAL GAGS:
  - Jessica filming while walking backward
  - Todd frantically wiping forehead
  - Maurice calmly closing briefcase
  - Derek grabbing all his folders (drops some)

**Beat 13: Ron's Calm Decision**
- ACTION: Ron stands slowly, everyone freezes
- BLOCKING: Ron center stage, others around him
- TIMING: Slow, deliberate contrast to chaos
- CAMERA: Wide shot showing everyone stopped

**Beat 14: Final Evacuation**
- ACTION: Everyone rushes to door stage right
- BLOCKING: Single file, Derek last
- TIMING: Quick exit
- PHYSICAL: Smoke visible near door

**Beat 15: Derek's Final Moment**
- ACTION: Derek stops at door, looks back
- BLOCKING: Silhouette in doorway
- TIMING: Brief pause
- CAMERA: Over-shoulder shot of empty boardroom

**Beat 16: Maurice's Return**
- ACTION: Maurice pokes head back through door
- BLOCKING: Just head visible
- TIMING: Beat after Derek exits
- CAMERA: Close-up on Maurice's face

**Beat 17: Blackout**
- ACTION: Lights cut to black
- TIMING: Immediately after Maurice exits
- SOUND: Alarm continues briefly into blackout

## PHYSICAL COMEDY NOTES

### Ron (CEO)
- **Falling asleep**: Head tilts back, mouth slightly open
- **Waking up**: Sudden jerk, coffee nearly spills
- **Confusion**: Squinting at screen, tilting head
- **Final decision**: Overly serious face for absurd statement

### Brenda (CMO)
- **Enthusiasm**: Big gestures, constant movement
- **Presentation**: Points at screen dramatically
- **Evacuation**: Still gesturing while leaving
- **Energy**: Never drops, even during fire

### Derek (Safety VP)
- **Stress eating**: Grabs snacks from table periodically
- **Phone checking**: Increasingly frantic
- **Folder slam**: Big, loud, physical
- **Defeat**: Slumped shoulders, head in hands

### Maurice (Legal)
- **Calmness**: Never rushes, even during alarm
- **Gestures**: Philosophical hand movements
- **Briefcase**: Methodically closes it during evacuation
- **Final look**: Deadpan delivery

### Todd (Engineering)
- **Sweating**: Constant forehead wiping, increases with stress
- **Nervousness**: Fidgeting with tablet
- **Technical speak**: Hands gesture wildly during jargon
- **Evacuation**: Most panicked movement

### Jessica (Junior Associate)
- **Note-taking**: Frantic typing on tablet
- **Enthusiasm**: Bouncing in seat
- **Filming**: Phone out, walking backward
- **Energy**: Matches Brenda's enthusiasm

## TIMING NOTES

### Pauses for Laughs
- After "We make ovens?" - 2 second pause
- After "Allegedly confirmed" - 1 second pause
- After "That's not a bug, that's a FEATURE!" - 2 second pause
- After "Customer satisfaction high among survivors" - 2 second pause
- After "Are we still talking about the toaster?" - 3 second pause
- After "We're calling it a feature" - 2 second pause
- After "What's a refrigerator?" - 2 second pause
- After final "Allegedly" - 1 second pause before blackout

### Rapid-Fire Sections
- Rebranding suggestions (Act 2) - overlapping dialogue
- Evacuation chaos (Act 3) - fast-paced movement

### Slow Sections
- Ron's final decision - deliberate, slow delivery
- Derek's impassioned speech - slower for emphasis

## SAFETY NOTES

- Smoke effect should be minimal and safe
- Coffee spill should be controlled (use water)
- Folder slam should be loud but safe
- Evacuation choreography should be rehearsed to avoid collisions
- Fire alarm sound should be loud but not damaging to ears
