# Beat Sheet - Self-Destruct Oven SNL Sketch

## Three-Act Structure Overview

**Total Runtime**: 3:30 - 4:00 minutes  
**Total Pages**: 3.5 - 4 pages  
**Major Laugh Moments**: 10

---

## ACT 1: SETUP (0:00 - 1:00)

### BEAT 1: Cold Open - The Meeting Begins (0:00 - 0:20)
**Scene**: Corporate boardroom. Everyone seated except Brenda who's standing at presentation screen.

**Action**:
- Ron (CEO) is half-asleep, coffee in hand
- Brenda enthusiastically welcomes everyone
- Derek looks stressed, shuffling papers nervously
- Jessica takes notes eagerly

**Comedy Moment**: 
- Ron wakes up confused: "Is this the refrigerator meeting?"
- Brenda: "No sir, the oven meeting."
- Ron: "We make ovens?"

**Purpose**: Establish setting, introduce characters, show CEO is clueless

---

### BEAT 2: The Problem Revealed (0:20 - 0:45)
**Action**:
- Derek tries to present safety data
- Shows statistics: "40% of our self-cleaning ovens have caused house fires"
- Room goes silent
- Derek: "We need to issue a recall immediately"

**Comedy Moment**:
- Jessica drops her pen in shock
- Maurice (Legal): "Alleged fires"
- Derek: "No, actual fires. Confirmed fires."
- Maurice: "Allegedly confirmed"

**Purpose**: Establish the central conflict clearly

---

### BEAT 3: The "Feature vs Bug" Debate Begins (0:45 - 1:00)
**Action**:
- Brenda stands up, takes control
- "Let's not be hasty. Is this really a problem, or is this an opportunity?"
- Derek: "It's definitely a problem!"
- Brenda: "That's not a bug, that's a FEATURE!"

**Comedy Moment**:
- Jessica immediately writes down "fire = feature" in her notes
- Todd (Engineering) wipes sweat from forehead
- Ron: "What's the difference between a bug and a feature?"

**Purpose**: Establish the absurd premise of the sketch

---

## ACT 2: ESCALATION (1:00 - 3:00)

### BEAT 4: Marketing Spin Begins (1:00 - 1:20)
**Action**:
- Brenda starts pitching marketing angles
- "We're not selling ovens that burn houses down..."
- "We're selling ovens that create opportunities for home renovation!"

**Comedy Moment**:
- Jessica: "Oh my god, that's literally brilliant!"
- Derek: "That's literally insane!"
- Brenda: "We could partner with home insurance companies!"

**Purpose**: Show how far they'll go to spin disaster as positive

---

### BEAT 5: Legal Loopholes (1:20 - 1:40)
**Action**:
- Maurice provides legal justification
- "Ze oven does not start ze fire. Ze house was already flammable."
- Pulls out precedent cases

**Comedy Moment**:
- Maurice: "In 1987, a toaster was ruled not responsible for burning toast"
- Derek: "That's completely different!"
- Maurice: "Is it though? Is it really?"
- Ron: "I like toast."

**Purpose**: Show legal enabling absurdity

---

### BEAT 6: Engineering Techno-Babble (1:40 - 2:00)
**Action**:
- Todd tries to explain the technical problem
- Uses increasingly complex jargon to avoid saying "it's broken"

**Comedy Moment**:
- Todd: "Well, technically, the thermal coefficient of the self-cleaning cycle creates an exothermic reaction that, technically, could theoretically interact with combustible materials in a, technically speaking, rapid oxidation event..."
- Derek: "You mean a FIRE!"
- Todd: "...technically, yes."

**Purpose**: Show how technical people hide behind jargon

---

### BEAT 7: Data Manipulation (2:00 - 2:20)
**Action**:
- Brenda starts reframing the statistics
- "Only 40% caused fires. That means 60% DIDN'T!"
- Jessica enthusiastically agrees

**Comedy Moment**:
- Brenda: "Customer satisfaction is very high among survivors!"
- Derek: "SURVIVORS?!"
- Jessica: "That's literally a great testimonial angle!"
- Derek's phone buzzes - another fire alert

**Purpose**: Peak absurdity of spin

---

### BEAT 8: The Rebranding Pitch (2:20 - 2:45)
**Action**:
- Brenda unveils new marketing campaign
- Suggests renaming "self-cleaning" to something else

**Comedy Moment**:
- Brenda: "What if we call it 'Thermal Reset Technology'?"
- Jessica: "Or 'Extreme Clean Mode'!"
- Maurice: "Or 'Controlled Combustion Event'!"
- Ron: "I like the one with the fire."
- Everyone: "Which one?"
- Ron: "Yes."

**Purpose**: Show committee absurdity, build to climax

---

### BEAT 9: Derek's Breaking Point (2:45 - 3:00)
**Action**:
- Derek stands up, slams folder on table
- Impassioned speech about safety and responsibility
- "People are losing their HOMES!"

**Comedy Moment**:
- Powerful moment of silence
- Ron: "Are we still talking about the toaster?"
- Derek sits down defeated
- Todd pats him on shoulder: "Technically, you're not wrong..."

**Purpose**: Moment of truth before the twist

---

## ACT 3: RESOLUTION (3:00 - 4:00)

### BEAT 10: The Twist - Fire in the Room (3:00 - 3:30)
**Action**:
- Smoke alarm goes off in the building
- Everyone panics
- Someone asks "What's burning?"
- Todd checks his phone: "Uh... the test kitchen... they were demonstrating the self-cleaning feature..."

**Comedy Moment**:
- Alarm blaring
- Everyone grabbing their things
- Brenda (still spinning): "This is fine! This is actually great content for social media!"
- Jessica filming on her phone: "This is literally going viral!"
- Maurice: "Allegedly on fire!"
- Derek: "I TOLD YOU!"

**Purpose**: Physical comedy, ironic twist, validates Derek

---

### BEAT 11: The Button - Final Decision (3:30 - 4:00)
**Action**:
- Everyone rushing toward door
- Ron stands up calmly
- Makes executive decision

**Comedy Moment**:
- Ron: "Alright, I've made my decision."
- Everyone stops, turns
- Ron: "We're calling it a feature."
- Derek: "WHAT?!"
- Ron: "Also, we're pivoting to refrigerators. Much safer."
- Brenda: "Brilliant!"
- Ron: "What's a refrigerator?"
- [Blackout as alarm continues]

**Purpose**: Absurd ending that reframes everything, shows nothing was learned

---

## Callback Structure

**Setup → Payoff**:
1. Ron thinks they make refrigerators → Final line about pivoting to refrigerators
2. Maurice says "allegedly" repeatedly → Says "allegedly on fire" during actual fire
3. Derek's phone buzzing with fire alerts → Test kitchen fire
4. Brenda's "feature not bug" catchphrase → Ron's final decision
5. Jessica filming everything → Films the actual fire for social media

---

## Escalation Arc

**Level 1**: Acknowledge fires exist  
**Level 2**: Spin fires as positive  
**Level 3**: Legal justification for fires  
**Level 4**: Technical obfuscation of fires  
**Level 5**: Rebranding fires  
**Level 6**: Actual fire happens  
**Level 7**: Still calling it a feature
