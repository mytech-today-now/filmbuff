# Proposal: Self-Destruct Oven SNL Skit

## Overview
Create a satirical Saturday Night Live sketch that lampoons corporate America's tendency to rebrand product failures as features, centered around an oven manufacturer's "self-cleaning" function that literally sets houses on fire.

## Concept
A boardroom meeting where oven manufacturing executives and subordinates debate whether their self-cleaning ovens—which heat to such extreme temperatures they catch fire and burn down houses—should be marketed as a "feature" or acknowledged as a "bug."

## Target Specifications
- **Duration**: 2.5 - 4 minutes
- **Format**: SNL-style sketch comedy
- **Tone**: Comedic, satirical, thought-provoking
- **Setting**: Corporate boardroom meeting

## Key Elements

### Characters
- Multiple distinct personalities with varied accents
- Clear hierarchy (executives vs. subordinates)
- Each character should represent different corporate archetypes

### Structure
- **Beginning**: Establish the meeting and the problem
- **Middle**: Escalating debate between "feature" vs "bug" camps
- **End**: Surprising, thought-provoking conclusion

### Themes
- Corporate doublespeak and spin
- Technology's unintended consequences
- Prioritizing profit over safety
- Absurdity of modern consumer products

## Success Criteria
- Captures SNL's comedic style and pacing
- Satirizes corporate culture effectively
- Characters are memorable and distinct
- Ending delivers both surprise and social commentary
- Runtime fits within 2.5-4 minute window

## Deliverable
Complete screenplay formatted for SNL production, including:
- Character descriptions
- Dialogue with stage directions
- Clear scene blocking
- Timing notes for comedic beats
