# Beat Sheet
## Opening Image
* The USS Reagan navigates through the depths of space, establishing the tone for the story

## Theme Stated
* The crew's daily duties and interactions hint at the importance of loyalty, duty, and sacrifice in the face of adversity

## Set-Up
* Introduction to the USS Reagan's crew and their mission
* Discovery of the decrepit spacecraft and the communists on board

## Catalyst
* The communists' plan to revolt against the USS Reagan's crew is revealed, setting the story in motion

## Debate
* The Star-captain weighs the pros and cons of taking drastic measures against the communists
* The crew is divided on the best course of action

## Break into Two
* The Star-captain decides to jettison the communists into space, marking a turning point in the story

## B Story
* The aftermath of the Star-captain's decision and the crew's reaction to it
* The bureaucratic red tape and intergalactic politics that follow

## Fun and Games
* Comedic misunderstandings and moments as the crew navigates the fallout
* The Star-captain's justification of his decision to StarFleet Command

## Midpoint
* The crew's division and debate over the Star-captain's decision reaches a peak
* The intergalactic fallout and controversy over the USS Reagan's actions begin to escalate

## Bad Guys Close In
* The consequences of the Star-captain's decision come back to haunt him and the crew
* The communists' allies and supporters in the galaxy begin to retaliate

## All is Lost
* The USS Reagan's crew is at a loss for how to navigate the complex web of intergalactic politics
* The Star-captain's decision is questioned and criticized by all sides

## Dark Night of the Soul
* The Star-captain and the crew are forced to confront the true meaning of loyalty, duty, and sacrifice
* The crew's unity and morale are tested

## Break into Three
* The crew comes to terms with the Star-captain's decision and the consequences that follow
* A new understanding and appreciation for the sacrifices made to protect the greater good emerge

## Finale
* The USS Reagan continues on its mission, with the crew forever changed by their experience
* The Star-captain's decision is left to the audience's interpretation, as the scene fades to black

## Final Image
* The USS Reagan sails off into the distance, a symbol of the crew's resilience and determination
* The theme of loyalty, duty, and sacrifice is reinforced, leaving a lasting impression on the audience
