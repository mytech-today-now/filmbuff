# Treatment
## Scene 1: Introduction to the USS Reagan
The scene opens with a sweeping shot of the USS Reagan, a stalwart vessel of StarFleet, navigating through the depths of space. The crew is busy with their daily duties, unaware of the impending danger that lies ahead.

## Scene 2: Discovery of the Decrepit Spacecraft
The USS Reagan's sensors detect a mysterious and decrepit spacecraft drifting aimlessly in space. The crew is alerted, and the Star-captain orders a team to investigate the strange vessel.

## Scene 3: Boarding the Decrepit Spacecraft
A team of crew members, led by the ship's first officer, boards the decrepit spacecraft, finding it in a state of disarray. They soon discover a group of communists, who have been secretly infiltrating the galaxy, spreading their ideology, and recruiting followers.

## Scene 4: The Communists' Plan
The communists, fueled by their radical beliefs, reveal their plan to revolt against the USS Reagan's crew, seeking to overthrow the established order of StarFleet and claim the ship for themselves. The crew is caught off guard, and tensions rise as the communists' rebellion gains momentum.

## Scene 5: The Star-Captain's Dilemma
The USS Reagan's Star-captain is faced with an impossible decision: risk the lives of his crew and the stability of the ship, or take drastic measures to maintain order and defend StarFleet against the communist threat. The Star-captain confers with his advisors, weighing the pros and cons of each option.

## Scene 6: The Decision to Jettison the Communists
With the fate of the ship and its crew hanging in the balance, the Star-captain makes the bold and controversial decision to jettison the communists into space, saving the USS Reagan from certain chaos and destruction. The crew is shocked and divided by the decision.

## Scene 7: The Aftermath
The communists are jettisoned into space, and the USS Reagan's crew must navigate the fallout of their captain's decision. The scene cuts to a series of comedic misunderstandings, bureaucratic red tape, and intergalactic politics, as the crew confronts the consequences of their actions.

## Scene 8: The Star-Captain's Justification
The Star-captain is called to justify his decision to StarFleet Command, facing a barrage of questions and criticisms. The Star-captain stands by his decision, arguing that it was necessary to protect the greater good.

## Scene 9: The Crew's Reaction
The crew of the USS Reagan is divided, with some hailing the Star-captain's decision as a heroic act of self-preservation, while others condemn it as a reckless and brutal abuse of power. The scene cuts to a series of comedic moments, as the crew members debate and argue among themselves.

## Scene 10: The Intergalactic Fallout
The USS Reagan's actions spark a galaxy-wide controversy, with various alien species and factions weighing in on the debate. The scene cuts to a series of comedic moments, as the crew navigates the complex web of intergalactic politics.

## Scene 11: The Conclusion
The USS Reagan continues on its mission, with the crew coming to terms with the true meaning of loyalty, duty, and the sacrifices that must be made to protect the greater good. The Star-captain's decision is left to the audience's interpretation, as the scene fades to black.
