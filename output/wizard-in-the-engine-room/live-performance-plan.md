## LIVE PERFORMANCE FEASIBILITY ANALYSIS

### Advantages of Live Performance
1. **Authentic SNL experience** - True to SNL format
2. **Audience energy** - Live laughter enhances comedy
3. **Actor adrenaline** - Live performance energy
4. **No post-production** - Immediate broadcast

### Challenges of Live Performance
1. **Complex VFX** - Green flames, warp core effects difficult live
2. **Safety concerns** - Floo powder effect with live audience
3. **Set changes** - Engine room to conference room transition
4. **No retakes** - Single chance to get it right
5. **Technical complexity** - Multiple effects synchronized live

---

## DECISION: PRE-TAPE FORMAT CHOSEN

**Rationale:**
- **VFX quality** - Green flame effect requires post-production for best visual
- **Safety** - Floo powder effect safer in controlled pre-tape environment
- **Editing flexibility** - Can optimize comedic timing in post
- **Visual effects** - Warp core, floating candles, sparks better in pre-tape
- **Set transitions** - Smoother with editing

**Approved by:** Director, Producer, Technical Director

## LIVE PERFORMANCE RISK ASSESSMENT

### High-Risk Elements
1. **Floo powder effect** - Fire hazard, requires safety officer
2. **Sparks effect** - Fire hazard, requires safety officer
3. **Set change** - Timing critical, could delay show
4. **VFX quality** - Live composite may not look as good as post

### Medium-Risk Elements
1. **Actor mistakes** - No retakes in live format
2. **Technical failures** - Lighting/sound cues could miss
3. **Audience reaction** - Could throw off timing

### Low-Risk Elements
1. **Blocking** - Well-rehearsed, actors confident
2. **Dialogue** - Memorized, actors prepared
3. **Costumes/Props** - Simple, functional, tested

---

## FINAL RECOMMENDATION

**Format:** PRE-TAPE (Chosen)

**Reasoning:**
1. **VFX quality superior** in post-production
2. **Safety easier to control** in pre-tape environment
3. **Editing allows optimization** of comedic timing
4. **No risk of live technical failure**
5. **Better final product** for audience

**Live Performance:** Documented as contingency, not executed
