# WIZARD IN THE ENGINE ROOM
## First Draft Screenplay
### SNL Sketch - Runtime: 3.5 minutes

---

**FADE IN:**

**INT. USS ENTERPRISE - ENGINE ROOM - DAY**

*The engine room looks like a typical Star Trek set, but with mystical additions: floating candles, a cauldron bubbling in the corner, and constellation charts mixed with star maps. WIZARD CLIF HIGH (70s, magnificent white beard, Starfleet robes with wizard hat, carrying an ornate staff) stands at the center, waving his staff dramatically.*

**CAPTAIN** (V.O. over intercom)  
Clif, we need warp 9 to Rigel VII. Make it so.

**CLIF**  
*(muttering, rolling his eyes)*  
"Make it so." Like I'm some kind of vending machine.  
*(raises staff, speaks clearly)*  
**Velocitas Maxima Stellarum!**

*The ship LURCHES forward. Magical sparkles swirl around the warp core.*

**HEIDI** (20s, attractive, wearing Starfleet engineering uniform with a wand holster on her belt) enters, carrying a PADD and looking concerned.

**HEIDI**  
Clif, the transporter's acting up again. Someone tried to beam down and ended up with their molecules scattered across three decks.

**CLIF**  
Did you try **Appareo Correctus**?

**HEIDI**  
I tried **Appareo**, but I forgot the **Correctus** part.

**CLIF**  
*(chuckling, moving closer)*  
That's why you're still in training, love.  
*(he touches her hand)*  
Here, let me show you again...

*They stand close together, his hand over hers on her wand. Romantic tension.*

**HEIDI**  
You know we're not supposed to—

**CLIF**  
I know. But some spells are worth the risk.

*They're about to kiss when the door WHOOSHES open. CAPTAIN (50s, stern, classic Starfleet) enters with FIRST OFFICER SPOCK-TYPE (pointy ears, raised eyebrow).*

**CAPTAIN**  
*(clearing throat loudly)*  
Am I... interrupting something?

*Clif and Heidi jump apart.*

**CLIF**  
Just... teaching her the finer points of molecular reconstitution, Captain.

**CAPTAIN**  
Right. And I'm teaching my yeoman the finer points of... *(pause)* Never mind. Clif, Heidi, we need to talk. Conference room. Now.

---

**INT. USS ENTERPRISE - CONFERENCE ROOM - CONTINUOUS**

*The Captain sits at the head of the table. Clif and Heidi sit across from each other, looking guilty. The First Officer stands.*

**CAPTAIN**  
Starfleet regulations are clear. Fraternization between senior officers and subordinates is prohibited.

**CLIF**  
With all due respect, Captain, I'm not technically an officer. I'm a... consultant.

**FIRST OFFICER**  
A consultant who controls every critical system on this vessel. The shields, the warp drive, the transporters, the structural integrity field...

**CAPTAIN**  
Exactly. Which is why this relationship is a problem. Clif, I'm recommending your retirement.

**CLIF**  
*(standing, angry)*  
Retirement?! I'm the best damn wizard in the fleet! I graduated top of my house at Hogwarts Starfleet Academy!

**HEIDI**  
*(standing)*  
This is ridiculous! We're both adults!

**FIRST OFFICER**  
*(calmly)*  
The needs of the many outweigh the needs of the few. Or the two.  
*(pause)*  
Can Starfleet even afford to let you retire, Wizard High? Without you, this ship is just an expensive metal tube.

**CAPTAIN**  
That's... actually a good point. Clif, you can stay, but the relationship ends. Heidi, you can transfer to another ship, or you can resign.

**HEIDI**  
*(looking at Clif, then back at Captain)*  
I... I choose to resign.

**CAPTAIN**  
Heidi, think about your career—

**HEIDI**  
I have. And I choose him.

**CLIF**  
*(smiling)*  
Well, if she's resigning... then so am I.

**CAPTAIN**  
*(panicking)*  
Wait, what? No! Clif, you can't—

**CLIF**  
*(pulling out a small pouch)*  
Oh, but I can, Captain. I've got 47 years of unused vacation time, and I'm taking it all. Right now.

*Clif throws FLU POWDER on the ground. Green flames erupt.*

**HEIDI**  
*(grabbing Clif's hand)*  
Where are we going?

**CLIF**  
Risa. I hear the beaches are lovely this time of year.

**CAPTAIN**  
WAIT! WHO'S GOING TO RUN THE SHIP?!

**CLIF**  
*(grinning)*  
Not my problem anymore!  
*(to Heidi)*  
Ready, love?

**HEIDI**  
Ready!

*They step into the flames together and DISAPPEAR in a swirl of green smoke and sparkles.*

**FIRST OFFICER**  
Captain... the warp field is collapsing.

*The ship SHUDDERS violently. Red alert klaxons blare.*

**CAPTAIN**  
*(grabbing the table)*  
Can anyone here cast a spell?!

**FIRST OFFICER**  
I am a Vulcan, Captain. We do not believe in magic.

**CAPTAIN**  
*(desperate)*  
Does ANYONE on this ship know magic?!

*Silence. The ship shakes harder.*

**ENSIGN** (O.S., over intercom)  
Captain! We're dropping out of warp! And the shields are down! And there's an asteroid field ahead!

**CAPTAIN**  
*(looking at camera, deadpan)*  
I should have just let them date.

*The ship LURCHES. Everyone falls. Sparks fly.*

**FADE TO BLACK.**
