# Production Format Plan
## "Wizard in the Engine Room"

---

## Format Decision: **LIVE WITH PRE-RECORDED EFFECTS**

### Rationale
- **Live Performance:** Maintains SNL's signature energy and timing
- **Pre-Recorded Effects:** Green screen/VFX elements shot in advance
- **Hybrid Approach:** Best of both worlds for this sketch

### Why Live?
1. Actor chemistry (Clif/Heidi romance) works better live
2. Comedy timing benefits from live audience energy
3. Physical comedy (ship lurching, people falling) more authentic
4. SNL format tradition

### Why Pre-Recorded Elements?
1. Magical effects (sparkles, green flames) need VFX
2. Warp core visuals require compositing
3. Floating candles safer as VFX than practical effects
4. Consistent quality for magical elements

## Stage Blocking Notes

### Key Movements
1. **Clif's Staff Work** - Actor needs rehearsal with prop staff for dramatic gestures
2. **Heidi's Entrance** - Smooth walk from door to center, natural chemistry
3. **Hand Touch Moment** - Choreograph for camera angle, must look natural
4. **Floo Powder Throw** - Practice timing with VFX cue
5. **Disappearance** - Actors step back into trap door or behind set piece
6. **Ship Lurch** - All actors practice falling in same direction on cue

### Safety Considerations
- **Practical Sparks:** Fire safety officer on set
- **Falling Actors:** Padded floor or rehearsed falls
- **Staff Prop:** Lightweight, won't injure if swung
- **Floo Powder:** Non-toxic theatrical powder

**FORMAT: Live sketch with pre-recorded VFX elements composited in real-time**

- Green screen panels for conference room windows
