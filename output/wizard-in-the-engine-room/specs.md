# Specifications: "Wizard in the Engine Room"

## Core Concept

**The Fundamental Premise**: Faster-than-light travel is scientifically impossible. The laws of physics make warp speed unattainable. Starfleet has known this for 200 years and will never develop the technology. Instead, they employ wizards to power all their "advanced technology"—warp drive, transporters, shields, everything. Without wizards, Starfleet would be grounded on Earth with no space program whatsoever.

This is not a temporary solution or a secret they're trying to fix—this is permanent. Magic is the only way to travel the stars, and Starfleet is completely dependent on wizards.

## Technical Specifications

### Duration
- **Target Length**: 2.5-4.5 minutes
- **Format**: Live sketch or pre-recorded segment
- **Pacing**: Fast-paced comedy with brief emotional beat before finale

### Setting Requirements

#### Primary Location: Engine Room
- Star Trek-style engine room set with warp core
- Modified to include magical elements:
  - Wizard's staff holder
  - Spell books on consoles
  - Potion ingredients near control panels
  - Floo powder container
  - Magical effects overlaying "technology"

#### Secondary Location: Hearing Room
- Starfleet hearing chamber
- Formal, tribunal-style setup
- Brief scene (30-60 seconds)

### Character Specifications

#### The Wizard (Lead)
- **Age**: Mid-70s (visibly older, but spry)
- **Appearance**:
  - Long white beard
  - Slightly stooped posture
  - Starfleet uniform modified with wizard robes
  - Pointed wizard hat with Starfleet insignia
  - Wooden staff
- **Personality**: Confident, legendary, passionate, defiant
- **Skills**: Master of all ship-operating spells
- **Status**: Best wizard in the fleet, irreplaceable
- **Age Dynamic**: Old enough to be the Captain's father

#### The Apprentice (Co-Lead)
- **Age**: Mid-20s (visibly young, fresh-faced)
- **Appearance**:
  - Attractive, professional, youthful
  - Junior Starfleet wizard uniform
  - Wand or smaller staff
- **Background**: Muggle-born witch
- **Personality**: Smart, funny, talented, quick learner, devoted
- **Skills**: Promising but still learning
- **Relationship**: Romantic partner to the Wizard (age-inappropriate May-December romance)
- **Age Dynamic**: Young enough to be the Captain's daughter

#### The Captain (Supporting)
- **Age**: 40s-50s (middle-aged, between Clif and Heidi)
- **Role**: Authority figure, antagonist
- **Personality**: By-the-book, disapproving, uncomfortable with age-gap relationship
- **Function**: Enforces Starfleet regulations against fraternization, calls out the age difference
- **Arc**: Attempts to separate the couple, loses both wizards
- **Hypocrisy**: Dismisses other unconventional relationships on ship (thruple, polygamists)

#### The First Officer (Supporting)
- **Age**: 30s-50s
- **Role**: Logical voice, Spock-type character
- **Personality**: Deadpan, logical, Vulcan-like
- **Function**: Undermines Captain's moral authority by pointing out ship's other unconventional relationships
- **Key Lines**: References thruple on Deck 12 and polygamist compound on Deck 27
- **Arc**: Delivers "needs of the many" quote, announces ship's collapse

#### Additional Characters (Optional)
- Starfleet crew members (reactions, witnesses)
- Ensign (voice only - panicked announcements)
- Bridge crew (for ship careening scene)

## Story Structure

### Act 1: The Reveal (60-90 seconds)
**Scene**: Engine room during normal operations
- Wizard casting spells to maintain warp speed
- Apprentice assisting with force field maintenance
- Exposition through dialogue revealing the truth: FTL travel is scientifically impossible
- Establish that Starfleet has known for 200 years they'll never have the technology
- Make clear that without wizards, Starfleet has no space program
- Establish their competence and chemistry
- Reference Hogwarts training program
- Mention lucrative Starfleet wizard contracts

### Act 2: The Discovery (45-60 seconds)
**Scene**: Engine room, intimate moment interrupted
- Romantic moment between Wizard and Apprentice
- Caught by crew member or captain
- Immediate consequences established
- Transition to hearing

### Act 3: The Hearing (60-90 seconds)
**Scene**: Starfleet tribunal
- Captain presents case against fraternization
- Wizard and Apprentice defend their relationship
- Captain's ultimatum: Wizard must retire, Apprentice must choose
- Apprentice chooses love over career
- Both resign on the spot

### Act 4: The Exit (30-45 seconds)
**Scene**: Return to engine room or hearing room
- Wizard and Apprentice throw down Floo powder
- Disappear in green flames/smoke
- Cut to: Bridge crew panicking as ship loses control
- Final visual: Ship careening through space
- **End card**: "Starfleet is currently hiring wizards. No experience necessary."

## Dialogue Requirements

### Key Exposition Points
- Faster-than-light travel is impossible
- Wizards make everything work (warp drive, transporters, force fields)
- Special Hogwarts house trains Starfleet wizards
- High pay for wizard positions
- Starfleet fraternization regulations
- Wizard's legendary status and irreplaceability

### Comedy Beats
- Technobabble replaced with spell names
- "Reversing the polarity" = "Casting the reversal charm"
- Transporter malfunction = "Apparition mishap"
- Warp core breach = "Spell backfire"
- References to both franchises' tropes

### Emotional Beats
- Genuine love between the couple
- Wizard's defiance of authority
- Apprentice's difficult choice
- Their mutual commitment

## Visual Effects Requirements

### Practical Effects
- Floo powder smoke/flames (finale)
- Wand/staff lighting effects
- Spell-casting gestures and movements

### Post-Production Effects (if pre-recorded)
- Magical energy around warp core
- Spell effects (sparkles, light beams)
- Ship careening in space (final shot)
- Green Floo powder flames

### Costume Requirements
- Starfleet uniforms modified with wizard robes
- Wizard hat with Starfleet insignia
- Long white beard (wig/makeup)
- Wizard staff (practical prop)
- Wands or smaller magical implements

### Props
- Spell books labeled with Star Trek terminology
- Potion bottles
- Floo powder container
- PADDs (Star Trek tablets)
- Warp core console (existing set piece)

## Audio Requirements

### Sound Effects
- Star Trek ambient engine sounds
- Magical spell sounds (whooshes, chimes)
- Floo powder whoosh
- Ship alarm klaxons (finale)
- Transporter sound (revealed to be magical)

### Music
- Star Trek theme (opening, possibly modified)
- Dramatic sting for discovery
- Emotional underscore for choice
- Chaotic music for ship careening

## Casting Considerations

### Ideal Cast Types
- **Wizard**: Older male actor with comedy chops, can play both silly and sincere
- **Apprentice**: Younger female actor, strong comedic timing, chemistry with lead
- **Captain**: Authority figure type, can play straight man
- **Host/Featured Player**: Could play any role depending on fit

## Production Notes

### Complexity Level
- **Medium**: Requires set modification, costumes, some effects
- **Feasible for SNL**: Uses existing sketch comedy production capabilities
- **Pre-tape recommended**: Allows for better effects and pacing control

### Alternative Live Version
- Simplified effects (practical only)
- Focus on dialogue and performance
- Floo powder exit as blackout/lights effect
- Ship careening described via dialogue or simple projection
