## PRACTICAL EFFECT PROPS

### 7. FLOO POWDER FLAME EFFECT

**Description:** Green flame effect for disappearance scene

**Method:** Flash paper + green flame powder (safe theatrical effect)

**Specifications:**
- **Material:** Professional flash paper + copper-based flame colorant
- **Activation:** Controlled ignition by effects technician
- **Safety Equipment:**
  - Fire extinguisher on set
  - Fire-resistant mat under effect
  - Safety perimeter marked
  - Effects supervisor present

**Alternative (Safer):** Green LED light panel + fog machine for "flame" effect

**Quantity:** Enough for 3-5 takes  
**Cost:** $100 (flash paper + colorant) OR $200 (LED/fog alternative)  
**Safety Notes:** REQUIRES fire safety officer on set, rehearsal required

## SAFETY CHECKLIST

### Pre-Production Safety Review

- [ ] All props inspected for sharp edges or hazards
- [ ] Electronic props tested for electrical safety (low voltage only)
- [ ] Fire effects reviewed by fire safety officer
- [ ] Floo powder tested for non-toxicity and cleanup
- [ ] Cauldron bubbling effect tested (no hot liquids)
- [ ] Floating candles rigging tested for weight capacity
- [ ] Staff weight tested for extended holding comfort
- [ ] All glass bottles secured with stable bases
- [ ] Emergency procedures established for fire effects
- [ ] Fire extinguisher placement confirmed
- [ ] First aid kit available on set
- [ ] Prop master briefed on all safety protocols

### Actor Safety Briefing

- [ ] Staff handling technique demonstrated
- [ ] Wand safety (no pointing at faces)
- [ ] Floo powder throwing technique (away from faces)
- [ ] Cauldron proximity safety (no leaning over)
- [ ] Glass bottle handling (careful placement)
- [ ] Emergency stop procedures explained

## PROP MASTER RESPONSIBILITIES

**Pre-Production:**
- Acquire/build all props per specifications
- Conduct safety inspection
- Organize prop storage and tracking

**During Rehearsal:**
- Supervise prop handling
- Make adjustments as needed
- Ensure actor comfort and safety

**During Shoot:**
- Maintain prop continuity
- Quick repairs/replacements
- Manage practical effects (Floo powder, cauldron)
- Coordinate with effects team

**Post-Production:**
- Inventory all props
- Return rentals
- Store hero props for potential reshoots

## NOTES

**Starfleet Insignia:** All Starfleet-branded props should use consistent insignia design (coordinate with costume department)

**Magical Aesthetic:** Props should balance "recognizable Star Trek" with "recognizable Harry Potter" - both elements clearly visible

**Camera Considerations:** Hero props (staff, wands, PADD) should look good in close-ups - higher detail/finish

**Backup Props:** All hero props that are handled extensively should have backups in case of damage

**Continuity:** Prop master should photograph all prop placements for continuity between takes
