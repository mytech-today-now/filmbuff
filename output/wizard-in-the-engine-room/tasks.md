- Sets must be ready before blocking rehearsals

- (If pre-tape) Filming must be complete before post-production begins

## Risk Management

### High-Risk Items
- **Floo powder effect**: Requires fire safety coordination
- **Chemistry between leads**: Critical for emotional beats to land
- **Runtime**: Must stay within 2.5-4.5 minute window
- **Effects complexity**: May need simplification for live version

### Mitigation Strategies
- Test all practical effects extensively before production
- Chemistry read during casting process
- Time script and rehearsals rigorously
- Have simplified backup plan for effects if needed
