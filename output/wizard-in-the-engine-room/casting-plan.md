# Casting Plan
## "Wizard in the Engine Room"

---

## Character Breakdown

### LEAD ROLES

#### 1. WIZARD CLIF HIGH
**Age:** Mid-70s (visibly older, but spry)
**Type:** Lead Role
**Screen Time:** 3 minutes (entire sketch)

**Character Description:**
- Legendary Starfleet wizard, best in the fleet
- Wise, powerful, but with a playful side
- Romantic lead in age-inappropriate May-December romance
- Physical comedy ability required
- Must handle prop staff convincingly
- Should look old enough to be the Captain's father

**Required Skills:**
- Strong comedic timing
- Physical comedy (spell casting gestures, staff work)
- Romantic chemistry with much younger actress (appropriate/professional)
- Authoritative presence despite age
- Can deliver both humor and heart
- Comfortable playing age-gap romance for comedy

**Ideal SNL Cast Member:** Alec Baldwin (guest), Steve Martin (guest), or Martin Short (guest)  
**Alternative:** Current SNL repertory player with character work experience

**Voice/Delivery:** Warm, authoritative, slightly theatrical (wizard-like)

---

#### 2. HEIDI (Apprentice Wizard/Engineer)
**Age:** Mid-20s (visibly young, fresh-faced)
**Type:** Lead Role
**Screen Time:** 2.5 minutes

**Character Description:**
- Young, talented muggle-born witch
- Smart, capable, but still learning
- Romantic lead in age-inappropriate May-December romance opposite Clif
- Earnest and genuine
- Strong moral compass (chooses love over career)
- Should look young enough to be the Captain's daughter

**Required Skills:**
- Romantic chemistry with much older actor (professional)
- Comedic timing (straight-woman role with funny moments)
- Physicality (wand work, falling during ship lurch)
- Emotional range (comedy to genuine emotion)
- Comfortable playing age-gap romance for comedy

**Ideal SNL Cast Member:** Chloe Fineman, Ego Nwodim, or Heidi Gardner  
**Alternative:** Featured player ready for larger role

**Voice/Delivery:** Bright, intelligent, warm

---

### SUPPORTING ROLES

#### 3. CAPTAIN
**Age:** 40s-50s (younger than Clif, older than Heidi)
**Type:** Supporting Role
**Screen Time:** 1.5 minutes

**Character Description:**
- Classic Starfleet captain (Picard-type)
- Stern but fair
- Frustrated by regulations vs. reality
- Uncomfortable with the age-gap relationship (calls out that Clif could be his father, Heidi his daughter)
- Hypocritical about ship's other unconventional relationships
- Delivers final punchline

**Required Skills:**
- Strong authority presence
- Deadpan comedy delivery
- Physical comedy (ship lurching, falling)
- Can play "straight man" effectively
- Comfortable with awkward age-gap comedy

**Ideal SNL Cast Member:** Kenan Thompson, Mikey Day, or Andrew Dismukes  
**Alternative:** Guest host with dramatic background

**Voice/Delivery:** Authoritative, measured, classic "captain" voice

---

#### 4. FIRST OFFICER (Spock-Type)
**Age:** 30s-50s
**Type:** Supporting Role
**Screen Time:** 1 minute

**Character Description:**
- Logical, Vulcan-like
- Delivers famous "needs of the many" line
- Points out ship's other unconventional relationships (thruple, polygamist compound)
- Raised eyebrow, deadpan delivery
- Straight-faced comedy
- Undermines Captain's moral authority with logic

**Required Skills:**
- Deadpan delivery
- Can maintain serious face during absurdity
- Physical stillness (Vulcan-like composure)
- Perfect comedic timing for listing absurd ship relationships

**Ideal SNL Cast Member:** Bowen Yang, James Austin Johnson, or Marcello Hernández  
**Alternative:** Any cast member with strong deadpan skills

**Voice/Delivery:** Flat, logical, emotionless (Spock-like)

---

#### 5. ENSIGN (Voice Only)
**Age:** Any  
**Type:** Voice Role  
**Screen Time:** 10 seconds (voice over intercom)

**Character Description:**
- Panicked junior officer
- Delivers bad news about ship systems failing

**Required Skills:**
- Voice acting
- Can convey panic convincingly

**Ideal SNL Cast Member:** Any available cast member  
**Alternative:** Production assistant or crew member

**Voice/Delivery:** Panicked, urgent

---

## Casting Priorities

### CRITICAL CHEMISTRY PAIRINGS
1. **Clif + Heidi** - Must have believable romantic chemistry despite significant age gap (professional, comedic May-December dynamic)
2. **Clif + Captain** - Must have good conflict dynamic; Captain should seem young enough to be Clif's son
3. **Captain + First Officer** - Must work as command team; First Officer undermines Captain with deadpan logic
4. **Age Dynamics** - Visible age differences are essential to the comedy (Clif: 70s, Captain: 40s-50s, Heidi: 20s)

## Audition/Chemistry Read Schedule

### Week 1: Initial Casting

**Monday:**
- Review headshots/reels for guest star options (Clif role)
- Reach out to agents for availability

**Tuesday:**
- Hold auditions for Heidi role (if not using current cast)
- Review current SNL cast availability

**Wednesday:**
- Auditions for Captain and First Officer (if needed)
- Narrow down choices

**Thursday:**
- **CHEMISTRY READ #1:** Top 2-3 Clif candidates with top 2 Heidi candidates
- Focus on romantic scene (engine room hand-touch moment)

**Friday:**
- **CHEMISTRY READ #2:** Selected Clif/Heidi pair with Captain candidates
- Focus on conference room confrontation scene

---

### Week 2: Table Read & Rehearsal

**Monday:**
- **FULL TABLE READ** with complete cast
- Director, writers, and producers present
- Read entire script start to finish
- Notes session immediately following

**Tuesday:**
- Script revisions based on table read (if needed)
- Begin blocking rehearsals

**Wednesday:**
- Blocking rehearsal (engine room scene)
- Prop work (staff, wands)

**Thursday:**
- Blocking rehearsal (conference room scene)
- VFX timing rehearsal (floo powder moment)

**Friday:**
- Full run-through with all cast
- Technical rehearsal (camera positions)

## Casting Recommendations

### OPTION A: Guest Star + SNL Cast
- **Clif:** Steve Martin (guest) - Perfect age, comedy legend, physical comedy skills
- **Heidi:** Chloe Fineman - Versatile, great chemistry, strong actress
- **Captain:** Kenan Thompson - Authority, comedy timing, SNL veteran
- **First Officer:** Bowen Yang - Excellent deadpan, physical comedy
- **Ensign (VO):** Ego Nwodim - Great voice work

**Pros:** Star power, proven comedy skills, audience draw  
**Cons:** Guest star scheduling, higher budget

- **Heidi:** Chloe Fineman or Heidi Gardner

- **First Officer:** James Austin Johnson - Excellent impressionist, deadpan
- **Ensign (VO):** Ego Nwodim

**Pros:** Balance of star power and ensemble, best chemistry potential
