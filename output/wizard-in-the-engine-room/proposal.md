# Proposal: "Wizard in the Engine Room" - SNL Skit

## Overview
A 2.5-4.5 minute Saturday Night Live skit that reveals the "true" secret behind Star Trek's warp drive technology: it's not science—it's magic.

## Concept
The sketch parodies Star Trek by revealing that faster-than-light travel is scientifically impossible—the laws of physics make warp speed unattainable. Starfleet has known this for 200 years, so they secretly employ wizards to make the Enterprise work. The engine room houses a legendary wizard in his mid-70s in Starfleet robes who casts spells to move the ship, maintain force fields, and operate the transporter. Without wizards, Starfleet would have no space program. He's in an age-inappropriate May-December romance with his apprentice in her mid-20s.

## Core Premise
- **The Fundamental Truth**: FTL travel is scientifically impossible; Starfleet will never have the technology
- **The Secret**: All "advanced technology" on the Enterprise is actually magic—and always will be
- **The Dependency**: Without wizards, Starfleet has no warp drive, no transporters, no shields—nothing
- **The Wizard**: A legendary Starfleet wizard in his mid-70s, the best in the fleet, irreplaceable
- **The Apprentice**: A talented young witch in her mid-20s, his romantic partner
- **The Age Gap**: 50-year age difference drives the comedy and conflict
- **The Training**: Special Hogwarts house trains Starfleet wizards and witches
- **The Conflict**: Forbidden romance between the wizard and his young apprentice (age-inappropriate)
- **The Hypocrisy**: Captain objects to age gap but ignores ship's thruple and polygamist compound
- **The Stakes**: When caught, they must choose between love and Starfleet—and Starfleet loses both wizards

## Story Arc
1. **Setup**: Reveal the wizard operating the "engine room" with spells
2. **Character Introduction**: Meet the legendary wizard and his talented young apprentice
3. **Romance Subplot**: Their forbidden relationship is discovered
4. **Climax**: Starfleet hearing threatens to separate them
5. **Resolution**: They choose love, resign, and disappear via Floo powder—ship careens out of control

## Target Audience
- Star Trek fans
- Harry Potter fans
- SNL viewers who enjoy genre parody
- Fans of workplace comedy and romantic subplots

## Tone
Satirical, affectionate parody that lovingly mocks both Star Trek's technobabble and Harry Potter's magical system while telling a surprisingly heartfelt story about choosing love over duty.

## Unique Selling Points
- Fresh crossover concept (Star Trek × Harry Potter)
- Subverts expectations of both franchises
- Balances comedy with genuine emotional stakes
- Visual comedy potential (wizard in Starfleet uniform, spell-casting in engine room)
- Timely relevance (both franchises remain culturally significant)

## Format
- Duration: 2.5-4.5 minutes
- Format: Single-scene sketch with possible cutaways
- Setting: Enterprise engine room, brief hearing room scene
- Cast: 4-6 actors (Wizard, Apprentice, Captain, possibly crew members)
