# Costume Design Plan
## "Wizard in the Engine Room"

---

## Design Concept

**Theme:** Fusion of Star Trek (sci-fi) and Harry Potter (magical) aesthetics  
**Color Palette:** Starfleet colors (red, gold, blue) + Wizard colors (deep purple, silver, mystical blue)  
**Style:** Recognizable parody elements with high-quality execution

---

## Character Costumes

### 1. WIZARD CLIF HIGH (Mid-70s)

#### Base Costume: Starfleet-Wizard Hybrid Robes

**AGE CONSIDERATIONS:**
- Costume should emphasize his age (70s) for comedy
- Slightly stooped posture accommodated in robe cut
- Comfortable for extended wear by older actor

**ROBE:**
- **Style:** Floor-length wizard robe with Starfleet insignia
- **Color:** Deep space blue with silver constellation embroidery
- **Material:** Heavy velvet or velour (looks magical, moves well)
- **Details:**
  - Starfleet delta insignia on chest (large, prominent)
  - Rank pips on collar (Admiral level - 4 gold pips)
  - Silver star patterns along hem and sleeves
  - Wide sleeves (traditional wizard style)
  - Hidden pockets for floo powder pouch
  - Cut to accommodate slight stoop without looking sloppy

**HAT:**
- **Style:** Classic wizard pointed hat with Starfleet modification
- **Color:** Matching deep space blue
- **Details:**
  - Starfleet insignia on front
  - Silver stars and moons embroidered
  - Slightly bent tip (character/personality)
  - Structured to stay on during movement

**BEARD:**
- **Style:** Long white wizard beard (Gandalf/Dumbledore style)
- **Length:** Mid-chest
- **Material:** High-quality theatrical beard (lace-front if budget allows)
- **Styling:** Flowing, well-groomed, majestic
- **Purpose:** Emphasizes age and wizard archetype

**ACCESSORIES:**
- **Staff:** 6-foot tall, ornate design
  - Top: Crystal or LED-lit orb
  - Body: Carved wood appearance (lightweight material)
  - Bottom: Metallic base
  - Starfleet insignia incorporated into design
- **Belt:** Leather with pouches for spell components
- **Boots:** Knee-high black boots (practical, wizard-like)

**UNDERGARMENTS:**
- Black or dark blue tunic (visible at neckline)
- Dark pants (for movement/comfort)

---

### 2. HEIDI (Apprentice Wizard/Engineer - Mid-20s)

#### Base Costume: Starfleet Engineering Uniform with Magical Elements

**AGE CONSIDERATIONS:**
- Costume should emphasize youth and contrast with Clif's age
- Fresh, clean, professional appearance
- Youthful energy in styling

**UNIFORM:**
- **Style:** Standard Starfleet engineering jumpsuit (TNG-era style)
- **Color:** Gold/yellow (engineering division)
- **Material:** Stretch fabric for movement
- **Details:**
  - Starfleet insignia on chest
  - Rank pips (Ensign - 1 gold pip, junior rank)
  - Name badge: "HEIDI"
  - Fitted but professional
  - Crisp, new-looking (emphasizes she's early in career)

**MAGICAL ADDITIONS:**
- **Wand Holster:** Leather holster on belt (like a phaser holster but for wand)
- **Wand:** 10-inch wooden wand, visible in holster
- **Spell Component Pouch:** Small pouch on opposite hip
- **Subtle Embroidery:** Tiny magical symbols on collar (barely visible)

**HAIR:**
- Pulled back in practical ponytail or bun
- Professional Starfleet appearance
- Youthful, fresh styling
- Small decorative hair clip (subtle magical element - star or moon)

**ACCESSORIES:**
- Black Starfleet boots (standard issue)
- PADD (tablet device) - carried in opening scene
- Communicator badge (standard Starfleet)

### 3. CAPTAIN

#### Base Costume: Classic Starfleet Captain Uniform

**UNIFORM:**
- **Style:** Command uniform (TNG-era)
- **Color:** Red/burgundy (command division)
- **Material:** Professional fabric, tailored fit
- **Details:**
  - Starfleet insignia on chest
  - Captain's rank pips (4 gold pips)

**ACCESSORIES:**

### 4. FIRST OFFICER (Spock-Type)

#### Base Costume: Starfleet Science/Command Uniform with Vulcan Elements

**UNIFORM:**
- **Style:** Science or command uniform (TNG-era)
- **Color:** Blue (science) or red (command) - director's choice
- **Material:** Standard Starfleet uniform fabric
- **Details:**
  - Starfleet insignia
  - Commander rank pips (3 gold pips)

**VULCAN ELEMENTS:**
- **Ears:** Pointed ear prosthetics (high quality)
- **Eyebrows:** Severe, angled (makeup)
- **Hair:** Straight, severe style (Vulcan bowl cut or slicked back)

**ACCESSORIES:**
- Black Starfleet boots
- Communicator badge
- Hands positioned in occasional Vulcan salute position

## Props List

### MAGICAL PROPS

1. **Clif's Staff**
   - Quantity: 2 (hero prop + stunt prop)
   - Features: LED lighting in crystal top (battery operated)
   - Weight: Lightweight (actor carries for 3+ minutes)

2. **Wands**
   - Heidi's wand: 1 hero prop
   - Background wands: 2-3 (set dressing)
   - Style: Harry Potter-inspired but unique designs

3. **Floo Powder Pouch**
   - Quantity: 2 (hero + backup)
   - Material: Leather or suede
   - Contents: Safe theatrical powder (non-toxic)
   - Drawstring closure

4. **Spell Books**
   - Quantity: 3-4 (set dressing)
   - Appearance: Leather-bound, aged
   - Titles visible: "Starfleet Enchantments Vol. 3", "Warp Drive Spells"

### STARFLEET PROPS

5. **PADDs (Tablets)**
   - Quantity: 3
   - Style: Star Trek-style tablets
   - Can be non-functional (just props)

6. **Communicator Badges**
   - Quantity: 4 (one per uniformed character)
   - Style: TNG-era combadge
   - Can be purchased or 3D printed

## Sourcing Plan

### TO PURCHASE

**Starfleet Uniforms:**
- Source: Costume rental house or Star Trek costume retailers
- Alternative: Commission from costume maker
- Budget: $200-400 per uniform
- Timeline: 2 weeks lead time

**Wizard Robe & Hat:**
- Source: Theatrical costume shop or custom-made
- Alternative: Modify existing wizard costume
- Budget: $300-500
- Timeline: 2-3 weeks for custom

**Beard:**
- Source: Professional theatrical supplier
- Type: Lace-front for best appearance
- Budget: $150-300
- Timeline: 1 week

### TO BUILD/MODIFY

**Staff:**
- Build in-house or commission from prop maker
- Materials: PVC pipe core, foam details, LED lights
- Budget: $100-200
- Timeline: 1 week

**Wand Holster:**
- Modify existing leather belt holster
- Budget: $30-50
- Timeline: 3 days

**Wands:**
- Purchase pre-made or commission
- Source: Etsy, prop makers, or DIY
- Budget: $20-50 each
- Timeline: 1 week

## Fitting Schedule

### Week 1: Measurements & Orders

- **Monday:** Clif - robe, hat, beard fitting
- **Tuesday:** Heidi - uniform fitting, wand holster adjustment
- **Wednesday:** Captain & First Officer - uniform fittings
- **Thursday:** Vulcan ear prosthetic test (First Officer)
- **Friday:** Staff prop test with Clif

### Week 3: Final Fittings & Adjustments
- **Monday:** All alterations completed
- **Tuesday:** Full costume test with all cast

- **Friday:** Final approval, backup costumes prepared
