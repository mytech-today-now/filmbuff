## Comedy Timing Revisions

### Recommended Changes

1. **Opening Exchange** - Add beat after "Make it so"
   - BEFORE: Immediate eye roll
   - AFTER: Clif pauses, sighs, THEN eye rolls (builds frustration)

2. **Transporter Malfunction** - Enhance visual
   - ADD: "Three decks. One guy's head is in the mess hall asking for coffee."
   - Gives audience a funnier mental image

3. **"Not my problem anymore!"** - Extend beat
   - Add Clif's mischievous wink before disappearing
   - Gives actor moment to land the joke

4. **Final Line Timing**
   - Captain's "I should have just let them date" needs to land JUST before the lurch
   - Timing: Say line → 1 second pause → LURCH
